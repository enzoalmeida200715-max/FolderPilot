## Project's reach and impact

FolderPilot is a new MIT-licensed tool that helps people organise local files
with a preview and an undo option. It aims to make everyday file management
more approachable without requiring accounts or paid services. The project is
just starting, so I don’t yet have download figures, an established community,
or evidence of wider adoption to report.

## How will you use the subscription for your project?

I’ll use Claude to understand and improve the code, fix bugs, expand tests,
and make the documentation clearer. My first priorities are testing the app
on Windows and improving the experience based on real user feedback.

## Other info

The initial version was built with AI assistance. I’m learning to maintain it
and plan to develop it openly, with a focus on simple features, clear
documentation, and preserving users’ files.
